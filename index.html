What we do

Turn problem solving into a competitive skill.

We meet at Carmel High School to learn competitive programming concepts, work through problems, and prepare for contests such as USACO and Codeforces.

01

Learn

Club lectures break down algorithms, data structures, implementation ideas, and contest strategy into approachable steps.

02

Practice

Build speed and confidence by solving problems, comparing approaches, debugging code, and learning from other members.

03

Compete

Apply what you learn in online and in-person programming competitions, from beginner-friendly rounds to advanced contests.

Weekly meetings

Just show up.

No complicated application process. Come to a meeting, meet the team, and start solving. Meeting reminders and event updates are posted in Discord.
